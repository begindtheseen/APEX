::: context one-to-one The regular meeting with your manager
A one-to-one (often written 1:1) is a regular private meeting between you and your manager, usually weekly or every two weeks, for 30 minutes or so.

It is your meeting more than theirs: the place to raise problems, ask for feedback and discuss your growth. Bringing a short list of topics makes it far more useful than waiting to be asked.
:::

::: context feedback-instrument The ways you find out how you are doing
During the program your feedback came from several sources: the reviewer, the gates, the estimate log, eval numbers. On hire day it is tempting to drop them all and rely only on your manager's occasional comments.

The point is to replace each one with a workplace version, such as code review comments, estimates against real tickets and 1:1 questions, so you never go months without knowing where you stand.
:::
