::: context burn-rate The industry word is "burn rate"
Startups call the money going out each month the **burn rate**, and runway is savings divided by burn. The test here is whether you know both numbers without looking, because a plan whose monthly cost is fuzzy has a deadline that is fuzzy too.
:::

::: context mock-interview What a mock interview is
A practice interview run by someone who plays the real interviewer — ideally someone who has actually hired engineers. They ask the questions a company would, then tell you honestly where your answers were weak. It is the cheapest place to fail an interview, which is why the program books one well before the real ones start.
:::
